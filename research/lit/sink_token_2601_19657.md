# One Token Is Enough: Improving Diffusion Language Models with a Sink Token

- **arxiv**: 2601.19657 (2026)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19657
- **Authors**: Zihou Zhang, Zheyong Xie, Li Zhong, Haifeng Liu, Yao Hu,
  Shaosheng Cao

## Summary

Proposes adding a dedicated sink token (self-attending, globally visible) to
stabilize the "moving sink phenomenon" in DLMs.

## Key claims relevant to our work

- Adopts the [[attention_sinks_dlm_2510_15731]] framing: sinks in DLMs are
  unstable; fix by inserting a structural sink token.
- **No per-head analysis. No mask attention characterization. No LLaDA-specific
  head clustering.**

## Overlap with our `mask_binder` finding

None directly. They modify training-time structure to suppress sink
instability; we observe a stable head-resolved pattern of mask binding in
LLaDA at inference. Orthogonal contributions.
