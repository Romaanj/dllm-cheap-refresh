# DLM-Scope: Mechanistic Interpretability of Diffusion Language Models via Sparse Autoencoders

- **arxiv**: 2602.05859 (Feb 2026)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05859
- **Models studied**: Dream-7B, LLaDA-8B

## Summary

First SAE-based mech-interp for DLMs. Studies feature steering, decoding-order
analysis, base→instruct feature transfer.

## Key claims relevant to our work

- **Zero attention-head analysis.** Entirely SAE-feature-centric.
- Confirms cross-version (base/instruct) feature stability — analogous to our
  cross-task head stability but on a different unit of analysis.

## Overlap with our `mask_binder` finding

None directly — different unit of analysis (SAE residual features vs
attention heads). But useful as the published baseline for "mech interp on
LLaDA": our head-level analysis is complementary and currently unexplored in
their framework.
