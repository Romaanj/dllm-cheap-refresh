# SparseD: Sparse Attention for Diffusion Language Models

- **arxiv**: 2509.24014 (Sep 2025)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24014
- **Models tested**: LLaDA-1.5 and Dream-7B-Instruct

## Summary

Training-free sparse attention for dLLMs. Pre-computes top-rho% q-k pairs
per head during early denoising steps and reuses the pattern for the rest.
Up to 1.50x speedup over FlashAttention at 64k / 1024 steps.

## Three observations (Sec 3.2)

1. "attention patterns vary significantly across attention heads, showing
   head-specific patterns"
2. "attention patterns in each head remain highly similar across denoising
   steps"
3. "early diffusion steps are crucial for language generation"

## Head-level analysis

- Visualizes heterogeneous patterns inside one model: column-wise (sink-like),
  sliding-window, mixed. Same heterogeneity reported for both LLaDA-1.5 and
  Dream-7B-Instruct (Figs 7-8 Appendix).
- **No named taxonomy, no fractions, no clustering.** Heads are treated
  individually; the only universal claim is "heads differ, but each head is
  stable over time."
- Sparse pattern selection is per-head (Eq 5-6); they explicitly reject
  AR-style fixed patterns.

## Overlap with our work

- **Confirms** head-specific patterns are universal across LLaDA + Dream and
  stable across steps -> validates our "head-resolved" methodology.
- **Gap they leave**: no semantic classification (mask/punct/sink/window),
  no fractions, no cross-task IoU, no cross-model comparison of *which kinds*
  of heads exist. They observed "column-wise" and "sliding-window" but did
  not name or count them.
- Closest published claim to our story; the missing piece is a *named*
  universal taxonomy + cross-model alignment.
