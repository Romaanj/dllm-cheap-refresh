# dKV-Cache: The Cache for Diffusion Language Models

- authors: Xinyin Ma, Runpeng Yu, Gongfan Fang, Xinchao Wang
- venue: arXiv preprint
- year: 2025 (submitted 2025-05-21)
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15781
- tags: [dllm-cache, delayed-caching, reuse-based]

## Key claims

- "Delayed and conditioned" KV caching for diffusion LMs.
- Two variants: dKV-Cache-Decode (near-lossless) and dKV-Cache-Greedy
  (more aggressive, some accuracy trade).
- 2-10× speedup on 7B-scale dLLMs.
- Exploits "different tokens have distinct representation dynamics".

## Method

Cache KV one denoising step after a token is decoded (delayed). This means
the cache always lags by one step but captures the more stable
post-confirmation state. Reuse-based caching at token granularity, uniform
across layers (likely).

## Why it matters here

**Reuse-based paradigm**, contrast to our refresh-only. dKV-Cache trusts
cached KV after a token is decoded. Our R26 showed this paradigm has issues
when extended to scope-based reuse. dKV-Cache's "delayed" trick limits the
reuse risk by waiting for stable state.

Different granularity (per-token, not per-(layer × position)) and different
trigger (decoded vs drift). Compatible with our work as a prior baseline
to compare against.
