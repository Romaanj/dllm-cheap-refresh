# Mask Tokens as Prophet: Fine-Grained Cache Eviction for Efficient dLLM Inference (MaskKV)

- **arxiv**: 2510.09309 (Oct 2025)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09309
- **Authors**: Jianuo Huang, Yaojie Zhang, Yicun Yang, Benhao Huang, Biqing Qi,
  Dongrui Liu, Linfeng Zhang

## Summary

Training-free KV-cache eviction for dLLMs (LLaDA, Dream). Uses **mask-query
attention as the scoring signal** to decide which *prompt* tokens to evict.
Adaptive per-head cache budget.

## Key claims relevant to our work

1. **Head heterogeneity is acknowledged** — they distinguish two archetypes:
   - "Information Head" — long-range prompt retrieval (low mask-attn).
   - "Structure Head" — low prompt dependency, plans discourse structure
     (high mask-attn).
   This is qualitatively analogous to our `mask_binder` vs `prompt_focus`
   split.
2. They define a per-head **Prompt Preference** metric (Eq. 14): mask-to-prompt
   attention ratio. This is essentially the same quantity we use to identify
   `mask_binder`, but used as an *eviction signal* on prompt keys, not as a
   target of mechanistic study.
3. **What they do NOT do**:
   - Report a specific fraction of heads (no "~20%" claim).
   - Layer-depth distribution of mask-attending heads.
   - Cross-task IoU of which heads are mask-binders.
   - Treat mask-attention as the **object** of mechanistic analysis — they
     treat it as a *signal* for compressing prompt KV.

## Overlap with our `mask_binder` finding

**Strongest overlap of any paper found.** They independently observed that
some heads attend more to mask than prompt and operationalized it. But:

- Their goal: use mask-attention scores to **evict prompt KV**.
- Our finding: **identify and stably characterize** a ~20% head population
  attending to mask K *from* mask Q; show cross-task IoU 73-79%; show layer
  depth concentration; use it to **add** mask K to cache (R_curr) rather than
  evict.

**Novelty preserved**: head population statistics, cross-task stability,
layer concentration, and the inverse intervention (cache mask, not evict
prompt). But we should cite MaskKV as the closest prior art and *clearly
distinguish* on these dimensions.
