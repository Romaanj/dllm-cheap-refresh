# DPad: Efficient Diffusion Language Models with Suffix Dropout

- **arxiv**: 2508.14148 (Aug 2025)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14148

## Summary

Training-free suffix dropping. Observation: attention to distant suffix mask
positions decays sharply; drop them.

## Key claims relevant to our work

1. **Aggregate** observation that distant suffix-mask attention is small.
2. **No per-head analysis** — explicitly aggregated "all heads" when measuring
   suffix attention.
3. No head clustering, no mask-binder identification, no cross-task IoU on
   heads.

## Overlap with our `mask_binder` finding

- **Complementary**: DPad shows aggregate suffix-mask attention is small. We
  show a head-resolved 20% subpopulation that attends specifically to *current
  block* mask (R_curr), not distant. The DPad result is consistent with our
  observation that distant mask doesn't help — but they miss the
  current-block binder structure.
- Useful citation for "aggregate attention to mask is small, but…"
